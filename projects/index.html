Journal publication

LITERAS

A multi-agent framework for biomedical literature review and citation retrieval. The public repository accompanies the 2025 paper in Computers in Biology and Medicine.

The paper evaluates reference retrieval and literature-review generation. Consult the publication for its comparison systems, evaluation design, and limitations.

Journal publication

Bedside command-following after acute brain injury

Analysis code for the study of recovery and instability of bedside command-following, published in Neurocritical Care.

The repository describes the analysis pipeline and access requirements for the source ICU datasets. Raw patient data are not distributed in the repository.

Preprint

LitBench

A benchmark for retrieval-grounded, multi-paper evidence synthesis in epilepsy, with a public repository maintained by Goldenholz Lab.

The linked medRxiv preprint tests identifying source papers, retrieving supporting passages, combining evidence, and abstaining when evidence is absent. The work is a research benchmark.
