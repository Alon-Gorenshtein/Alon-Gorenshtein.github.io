Neuro-Copilot: AI for neurological decisions in the emergency department

Neuro-Copilot is a research approach to supporting neurological care in the emergency department, bringing language models and predictive methods to clinical documentation and decision support.

Research question

Can an AI-assisted approach help interpret clinical information and support decisions for patients presenting with neurological concerns?

Approach and evidence

In a 2025 Journal of Clinical Medicine paper, Gorenshtein and colleagues studied an AI-based clinical decision-making tool for neurologists in the emergency department using a retrospective dataset.

The study investigated predictions related to admission and agreement with expert decisions. Its authors describe the need for additional real-world and prospective evaluation of combined clinician–AI performance.

Authors and contributions

Alon Gorenshtein is first author. The publication credits Gorenshtein and Shahar Shelly with conceptualization and methodology alongside a multidisciplinary research team.

Research context

This was a retrospective research study, not evidence that the system independently provides safe clinical care. Consult the publication for study design and limitations.
