About

Medicine is
human work.

I’m Alon Gorenshtein (אלון גורנשטיין), a physician-scientist and AI engineer working at the intersection of neurology, clinical research, and machine intelligence.

At Harvard Medical School and Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, I study how AI systems can support clinical judgment, how we evaluate them, and how they behave in real-world settings. I also lead AI engineering at BRIDGE GenAI Lab.

Research projects

Ideas into
practice.

Tools and benchmarks built with collaborators to investigate clinical AI and neurological care.

01

INSPIRE

Research project

Alon Gorenshtein and collaborators developed INSPIRE, an agent-guided research system for interpreting and reporting nerve conduction studies and electromyography.

↗02

LITERAS

Research project

LITERAS is a multi-agent biomedical literature retrieval and review framework developed by Alon Gorenshtein and colleagues using PubMed-based search and citation validation.

↗03

LitBench

Research project

LitBench is a research benchmark by Alon Gorenshtein and colleagues for retrieval-grounded, multi-paper evidence synthesis in epilepsy.

↗04

Neuro-Copilot

Research project

Neuro-Copilot is a clinical AI research framework studied by Alon Gorenshtein and colleagues for neurological decision support in the emergency department.
